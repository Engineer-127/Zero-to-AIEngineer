Rules that matter more than any course

  1. 70% building, 30% watching. A video you watched is not a skill. The project at the end of each week is the real test.
  2. Push everything to GitHub. One repo per project with a README, a screenshot and a live demo link where possible.
  3. Don't skip Phases 1–3 to reach Gen AI. You'll hit a bug in week one of LLM work that only fundamentals can fix.
  4. Use AI as a tutor, not a ghostwriter. Ask it to explain and review. Type the code yourself while learning.
  5. Behind schedule is fine. The order matters; the dates don't. Stretch a week into two when a topic is hard.
  6. Write about what you learn. A short LinkedIn post per phase builds your profile and cements the concepts.
